THE MATERIAL

Belgian linen.
Nothing else.

Every piece in the SS25 collection is cut from a single Belgian linen mill — a family mill in Kortrijk that has been weaving since 1887. The weight is 210gsm. The weave is plain. The finish is unwashed — it softens with wear.

We do not use blends. We do not use synthetics. The hand feel is everything. The hand feel is the product.

THE PROCESS

How a piece is made.

01

The sketch

Each piece begins as a pencil sketch on A3 cartridge paper. No CAD. No digital tools. The sketch takes two to four days.

02

The toile

A test garment in calico. Fitted on a stand. Adjusted by hand. The toile is remade until the proportion is exact. This takes between one and three weeks.

03

The cut

Fabric cut by hand from a single-ply. No marker. No machine cutting. Each piece of linen is handled individually.

04

The stitch

French seams throughout. Interior construction matching exterior specification. 147 hand stitches per hem. The inside is as finished as the outside.

05

The finish

Each piece is steamed, inspected, and numbered. A production number is hand-stamped on the interior label. The piece is wrapped in unbleached tissue and held until the season opens.
